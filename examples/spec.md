# Specification

## SCN-001 Standard purchase

### CASE-001 Standard amount

Apply the member discount when a standard purchase satisfies the eligibility conditions.

### CASE-002 Minimum amount

A purchase of one unit of currency remains eligible when it satisfies the conditions.

## SCN-002 Discount cap

### CASE-001 Amount above cap

Apply the configured maximum discount when the calculated discount exceeds the cap.
